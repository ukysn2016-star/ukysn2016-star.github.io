II. New Words （3）

rug/rʌɡ/n.
a piece of thick material like a small carpet that is used for covering or decorating part of a floor
小地毯；垫子
Origin古挪威语 rogg（粗毛织物、毛毡），与古英语 rūg（粗糙的）相关；区别于 carpet（大地毯）
Forms
rugs（复数，小地毯）；rugged（形容词，崎岖的、粗犷的，同源异义）；rugby（名词，橄榄球，同源）
Colloc.a worn rug（破旧的小地毯）；on the rug（在地毯上）；pull the rug（俚语：拆台）

hindquarters/ˈhaɪndˌkwɔːtə(r)z/n.
the back part of a four-legged animal, including its two back legs
（四足动物的）后臀及后腿，作整体看待；日常和文学中常用复数。
Origin复合词：hind（后面的）+ quarters（部位），源自拉丁语 quartarius（四分之一）；指动物后四分之一躯体
Forms
单单数 hindquarter — 指一侧后躯／一条后腿，多用于解剖、屠宰等专业语境，日常几乎不用。e.g. a hindquarter of beef（半扇牛后躯肉）
复复数 hindquarters（文中用法）— 动物的后臀及后腿这一整体，常用复数，类似 trousers / scissors。
小结：日常与文学一律用复数 hindquarters 表“整体后半身”；单数仅特指某一条后腿或一侧后躯。
Colloc.the hindquarters of a leopard（豹子的后臀）；kick with hindquarters（用后腿踢）

contemplate/ˈkɒntəmpleɪt/v.（未在大纲中）
to think about something seriously and at length; (formal/literary) to look at somebody or something steadily and thoughtfully
沉思；深思熟虑；考虑（做某事）；（书面语）凝视，注视
Origin源自拉丁语 contemplari（凝神注视、专注地观察），由 com-（加强）＋ templum（供观察的空旷场地，后指神庙）构成，与 temple（神庙）同源；词义由“凝神注视”引申为“沉思”
Forms
contemplation /ˌkɒntəmˈpleɪʃn/（名词，沉思、冥想）；contemplative /kənˈtemplətɪv/（形容词，沉思的、好思索的）
Colloc.contemplate the statue（凝视雕像）；contemplate doing sth（考虑做某事，后接动名词）；contemplate the meaning of life（思索人生的意义）

IV. Paraphrase （6）

Para 5 · Para 6 · Para 7 · Para 8 · Para 9 · Para 10
Para 5
The headmaster would look at me and say,
The headmaster would fix his eyes on me and then begin to speak.
Notes
  • would 表过去习惯（used to），不是将来时。
Para 6
“What are we going to do with you?”
How are we going to deal with / handle you and your misbehaviour?
Notes
  • we 是校长站在校方立场上的口吻（而非真的指“我们两人”）。
  • 反问式责问，语气透出无奈与恼火。
Para 7
Well, what were they going to do with me? I would writhe my shoe some more and stare down at the worn rug.
I wondered what they would do to me; I rubbed one shoe against the other more nervously and kept my eyes on the shabby rug.
Notes
  • writhe one shoe over the other 描写紧张时的蹭鞋小动作。
  • worn = 用旧的、破旧的（不是“穿着的”）。
Para 8
“Look up, boy! Can’t you look up?”
Raise your head, boy! Can't you lift your eyes from the floor?
Notes
  • 此处 look up = 抬头（不是“查阅”义）。
  • Can’t you …? 是带责备意味的反问。
Para 9
Then I would look up at the cupboard, where the naked lady was frozen in her panic and the muscular gentleman contemplated the hindquarters of the leopard in endless gloom. I had nothing to say to the headmaster. His spectacles caught the light so that you could see nothing human behind them. There was no possibility of communication.
I would raise my eyes to the cupboard: the statue of Venus stood frozen in panic, and the muscular Thinker brooded over the leopard’s hindquarters in ceaseless gloom. I was speechless before the headmaster; his glasses reflected the light so that no human feeling showed behind them — we could not communicate at all.
Notes
  • frozen in her panic 以雕像的静止喻“凝固在惊恐中”。
  • caught the light 指镜片反光、遮住眼神。
  • nothing human behind them 暗示校长的冷漠与非人化。
Para 10
“Don’t you ever think at all?”
Don't you ever engage in any thinking whatsoever?
Notes
  • ever … at all 强化否定，表“全然不”。
